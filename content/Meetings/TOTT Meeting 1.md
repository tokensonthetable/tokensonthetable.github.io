### Queryable knowledge bases



**What to call it.** You'll find "LLM wiki", "LLM knowledge base", "Second brain". 
Until one name takes the clear lead, I like QKB: **queryable knowledge base**.

**A small example.** Imagine a knowledge base on one region's home cooking. It's narrow enough that OpenAI or Anthropic will never train it into their models, and that's the point.

**The advanced approach** would be training an open-source LLM on the material. That's hard, and overkill.

**Your own QKB.** There's real potential here for your teamwork and your classes, preproduction for example. A queryable knowledge base is a collection of your project's notes, plans, and research, organized so you can ask an AI questions about them. For a game team, that might mean asking *"What confused players in our last three playtests?"* or *"Why did we decide to cut that feature?"* and having the AI look through the team's own documents for answers. Those files stay available for you to read, revise, and share as the project grows, which gives future conversations a shared starting point. The AI can help you connect ideas and keep a record of decisions. You and your team still decide what to make.

## Tips and questions, throughout

These get mixed in between the demos, projected as text and posted in the Tokens on the Table Discord.

**Which AI**

- The frontier LLMs are ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
- All of us can get Codex through USC ([request access](https://itservices.usc.edu/2026/02/26/new-features-now-available-in-uscs-chatgpt-edu-workspace/)). That's worth $20 a month. (I find $20 a month for Claude deeply worth it.)
- Gemini can do persistent workspaces for free. Paying makes it better, but the other companies' free tiers can't do it at all.
- The companies' "CLIs" are Claude Code, (ChatGPT's) Codex, and (Gemini/Google's) Antigravity.
- There are many Chinese "open weight" models, meaning the model itself is published and you can download it. This is a huge topic.

**How to use it**

- Get to know your LLM's "effort": how much thinking it does before it answers.
- Get to know "Plan Mode," where the AI proposes a plan before it touches anything.
- Google Search, AI Mode, and Gemini are not the same thing.

**Research**

- Perplexity is the first alternative to Google search I've ever used.
- For deep research, ask Claude to draft the prompt for Perplexity.

**A question to sit with**

- If you build a QKB in, say, Claude, which qualities of the experience of using it carry over to Codex (including a good Codex model), and which don't?
